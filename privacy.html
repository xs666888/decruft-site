Decruft — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Decruft does not collect any data. It has no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts, and no third-party SDKs. The app ships without the network entitlement, so it cannot open a network connection at all — nothing it scans, reads, or removes can leave your Mac.

What stays on your Mac

To do its job the app stores a few things locally, inside its own sandbox container. None of it is transmitted anywhere:

  • Your settings — language, scan preferences, and the folders you have chosen for the large-file and duplicate scans.
  • Recently removed items — anything the app removes is moved into its own container and kept recoverable for 14 days, then deleted.
  • Purchase state — a local record of whether the one-time unlock has been purchased, kept in the system keychain.

Removing the app removes all of it.

What the app reads

Scanning reads file and folder names, sizes, and modification dates. It does not read the contents of your documents. Folders outside the caches it knows about are only examined when you pick them yourself in a standard macOS open panel.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether the purchase exists; the developer never sees your payment details, your Apple Account, or your name. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is a utility with no user-generated content, no messaging, and no advertising, and collects nothing from anyone regardless of age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.

Decruft — 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 10 日

Decruft 不收集任何数据。没有统计分析，没有崩溃上报，没有账号， 也没有任何第三方 SDK。应用在打包时不含网络权限，因此根本无法建立网络连接—— 它扫描、读取或删除的任何内容都不会离开你的 Mac。

留在你本机的内容

为了正常工作，应用会在自己的沙盒容器里保存少量数据，这些数据不会被发送到任何地方：

  • 你的设置——语言、扫描偏好，以及你为「大文件」和「重复文件」 扫描所选择的文件夹。
  • 最近删除——应用删除的内容会被移入它自己的容器，保留 14 天内 可还原，之后清除。
  • 购买状态——一次性解锁是否已购买的本地记录，保存在系统钥匙串中。

删除应用即删除以上全部内容。

应用读取的内容

扫描读取文件和文件夹的名称、大小和修改时间，不读取你文档的内容。 除了它已知的那些缓存目录之外的位置，只有在你通过 macOS 标准的打开面板亲自选择后才会被检查。

购买

一次性解锁通过 Apple 的 App 内购买销售。付款由 Apple 处理，Apple 只告知应用该笔购买是否存在； 开发者不会看到你的付款信息、Apple 账户或姓名。Apple 对该交易的处理适用 Apple 隐私政策。

儿童

本应用是一个工具类应用，不含用户生成内容、消息功能和广告， 不论用户年龄都不收集任何信息。

变更

本政策如有变更，更新后的版本会发布在本页面，并更新上方日期。
